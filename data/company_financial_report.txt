Acme Corporation - Q3 2024 Financial Summary

Revenue
Total revenue for Q3 2024 was $142.5 million, representing a 12% increase compared to Q3 2023 ($127.2 million). Revenue breakdown by segment:
- Enterprise Solutions: $89.3 million (63% of total)
- Cloud Services: $35.6 million (25% of total)
- Professional Services: $17.6 million (12% of total)

Operating Expenses
Total operating expenses were $118.7 million:
- Research and Development: $42.3 million (36% of revenue)
- Sales and Marketing: $38.1 million (27% of revenue)
- General and Administrative: $22.4 million (16% of revenue)
- Cost of Revenue: $15.9 million (11% of revenue)

Net Income
Net income for Q3 2024 was $18.2 million, up from $12.8 million in Q3 2023. Net profit margin improved to 12.8% from 10.1% year-over-year.

Key Metrics
- Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): $520 million
- Customer Retention Rate: 94.2%
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU): $4,850/year
- Total Customers: 12,400
- Employee Count: 5,100

Cash Position
Cash and cash equivalents: $285 million
Total debt: $45 million
Free cash flow: $24.6 million

Growth Outlook
Management expects Q4 2024 revenue between $148 million and $155 million, driven by strong demand for the new AI-powered analytics platform launched in September 2024. The company plans to hire 200 additional engineers in Q1 2025 to support product development.

The board of directors declared a quarterly dividend of $0.35 per share, payable on November 15, 2024 to shareholders of record as of October 31, 2024.
